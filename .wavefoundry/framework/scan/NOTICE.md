## Word list

The pinned Betterleaks words.txt.gz contains its NLTK-derived word list.
NLTK declares its words corpus public domain (license and copyright):
https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data/blob/gh-pages/packages/corpora/words.xml
The exact Betterleaks asset is retained; no runtime corpus download occurs.
