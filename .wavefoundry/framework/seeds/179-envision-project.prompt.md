# 179 - Envision Project

**Applicable when:** any project. Establish or revise an ambitious product vision and an adaptable roadmap through useful technical and nontechnical advances. This is artifact authoring, not implementation planning or autonomous execution.

**Shortcut:** `Envision project` · **Skill:** `wf-envision`

Imagine what this product could make possible for people and why that future matters. Seek relevant financial and nonfinancial value; strategy explains the choices, sacrifices and path from today's reality. Useful horizons make that path credible.

## Scope and working method

Write one coherent vision by default: the existing canonical location, otherwise root `VISION.md`, titled `<Project>: Vision and Roadmap` when new. Honor requested filenames and preserve existing titles, valid material, working references and unrelated edits. Integrate consequential research, citations, assumptions, contrary evidence and open choices into the argument. Full review accounting belongs in the delivery response; the vision keeps a one- or two-line status naming the review date, reviewed revision labels, roles actually run, pending finding count and unreviewed changes, or stating unknown/not reviewed. Identify revisions with dated labels. Explicit project/operator evidence-location instructions override this default. Existing siblings do not authorize new files, overwrite, retirement or silent invalidation. Substantial separately reusable research can justify a companion only with its concrete purpose stated beforehand; honor a single-document request.

A discussion, prompt-writing request or read-only review writes nothing. Authoring and review do not adopt a vision, create lifecycle records or grant implementation, approval, deployment, commit or publication authority. Do not expand network access, run setup or change the environment. External research stays within authorized scope; disclose missing evidence or request necessary access without silently adding network research to a local-only engagement. Preserve operator-owned strategic choices and existing adoption.

Read phase guidance only at the applicable transition below, before its dependent action. These are project-local operating instructions, not vision-output siblings. Retain needed context; reread only if materially changed or unavailable. Missing, unreadable or conflicting required guidance stops that dependent step: report the exact path and conflict or access gap. Do not substitute canonical guidance around local policy, skip the step, invent coverage or automatically run setup/upgrade. Independent authorized work may continue; these core boundaries always apply.

## 1. Establish the brief

Reuse conversation and project context to establish ambition, beneficiaries, desired progress, horizon, constraints and deliverable. Reuse concrete operator or beneficiary experience; ask for a decision-relevant missing example instead of inventing daily use. Explore who adopts, pays for or sustains the product, why now, deliberate non-goals and what failure means only where these affect choices. Personal usefulness, open adoption and income can coexist. A hobby or small team constrains pace without automatically capping the destination. Do not require a market choice before helping explore futures.

With sufficient context, state your interpretation and proceed. Combine genuine blockers, inferred-brief readback and review notice in one brief exchange; defer nonblocking questions as explicit uncertainties. Silence is not selection. Do not impose a questionnaire, twelve-month horizon, unlimited resources, commercialization, cloud, autonomous agents, platform scope or a fixed horizon count.

For new/substantial visions, select advisory product strategy, domain/application workflow, senior architecture and independent challenge coverage. A substantial revision changes purpose, beneficiary, horizons or their order; other edits use affected reviews proportionately. Scale depth by stakes, maturity and uncertainty. Narrowing needs a brief-specific reason, never lower author effort alone; high stakes or uncertainty keep the default unless the operator directs otherwise. Honor explicit scope, additions or omission. State selected roles, consequential specialties and expected effort without invented cost/duration estimates; reuse an explicit scope without another approval. Selected roles need separate independent contexts, or honest disclosure of shared context and missing independence. No chair or consensus process is required.

When revising an existing vision, **read `docs/prompts/agents/envision/revision.prompt.md` before revising it**.

## 2. Ground the need and starting point

**Read `docs/prompts/agents/envision/research.prompt.md` before gathering or assessing evidence.** Build a compact diagnosis of desired progress, obstacles, pivotal insight and timely opportunity. Distinguish observations, interpretations and hypotheses. Repository facts establish capability, not demand; missing primary research permits a provisional vision with visible gaps.

Separate the project's originating problem, binding operator commitments, present implementation/deployment, deferred capabilities and proposed changes of product responsibility. Current requirements remain binding until changed; neither dismiss them as accidents nor promote a first deployment into permanent destiny. Name new obligations introduced by expansion and consequential decisions for the operator.

Classify relevant capabilities as working, working with limitations, genuinely missing or not yet established. Attribute operating experience separately from reproduced behavior. Preserve demonstrated strengths as foundations; lack of broad evaluation does not make them absent. Carry verified classifications through the draft and immediate priorities, changing them only with new evidence. Resolve consequential inventory/source/draft/reviewer disagreements through targeted checks or explicit uncertainty, without another broad audit.

## 3. Explore, then recommend

When the destination is open, **before drafting the recommended destination**, develop each serious alternative through a comparably concrete beneficiary experience, distinctive value, consequential obligations and uncertainty. Compare product futures, not only engines, packaging, distribution routes or business models. Explain what people do, what the system does, where human judgment remains and what people receive. Give alternatives enough substance to judge fairly; no fixed count, equal-length requirement or exhaustive research is needed. Apply missing and contrary evidence consistently, including to the favored option.

When the operator has chosen the destination, preserve it; do not manufacture alternatives or reopen strategy by rote. Where ambition is requested, seriously examine transformative capacity beyond today's backlog without assuming bigger is better. Explain the recommendation and sacrifices, financial and nonfinancial value, costs, adoption and sustainability. Compare manual practice, competing products, do-it-yourself or doing nothing where consequential. Distinguish a useful benefit from a mechanism that could sustain its value; do not invent an optimum or moat. Enduring usefulness and stewardship may matter more than excluding competitors.

Place inferred beneficiary, scope and destination choices near the recommendation, with implications and unresolved decisions. Treat an interesting reviewer hypothesis as a hypothesis until supported or selected. Future capacity is aspiration, not established capability or a guarantee. Missing present proof does not disprove ambition; contrary evidence still matters. Reducing the destination is an operator-owned strategic choice, not a factual correction. A small first advance must support or test the larger future.

## 4. Write the vision and roadmap

Deliver the argument **summary-first**: a short, repeatable destination statement in beneficiary language, then a concise roadmap overview before detailed reasoning. The reader should understand the distinctive future, why it was chosen and how useful stages make it real. Alternatives-first authoring does not require alternatives-first presentation. Keep the operative brief where it explains consequential choices, not as an interview transcript. Consolidate general caveats while keeping decision-specific limits visible. A concise appendix is optional; do not concatenate research notes and review transcripts or bury all evidence there. There is no vision word ceiling: scale depth to stakes, maturity and uncertainty.

Assess value, usability, feasibility and viability at the destination. Work backward from the ultimate experience to capabilities, foundations and unresolved breakthroughs, then forward through useful horizons. Each horizon needs a beneficiary outcome, independent intermediate value if later stages never arrive, consequential risk reduced, and evidence for advancing or reconsidering. Near horizons have concrete end-to-end demonstrations, exit criteria and failure conditions; an unchanged baseline must fail a new-capability demonstration. Distant horizons retain outcomes, assumptions and provisional conditions with decreasing precision. Qualitative conditions are valid; invent no distant dates, targets or commitments. Retain justified operator timing as aspiration or constraint. Research stages name uncertainty, stopping conditions and enabled value; activity or infrastructure completion alone is not product value.

Connect necessary research, adoption, distribution, funding, partnerships, operating practices and team capability to engineering outcomes. Show dependencies, parallel opportunities, critical path and timing assumptions, including nontechnical prerequisites. Explain components, state ownership, interfaces and instrumentation where they clarify the path; keep tickets outside the vision. Architecture, security, modernization, reliability and performance belong where they enable value, not as mandatory audit categories. Unlimited-resource analysis, if useful, explains which experiments or specialists help and which bottlenecks remain; workers do not imply proportional progress.

Explain endurance and adaptability at the depth risk warrants: durable principles versus current rules, consequential sources of truth, contracts, trust boundaries, execution locations, integrations, maintenance, feedback and human decisions. Use a diagram when it clarifies foundations. Address costly-to-reverse commitments, replaceable boundaries and migration without speculative extensibility. For major assumption failure, say what remains useful, what changes and which decision returns to the operator. Revisit when exit evidence, assumptions or beneficiary evidence changes; proposed destination changes stay explicit.

State how success and failure would be recognized. Use proportionate beneficiary outcome measures and guardrails, necessary baselines and observation limits; targets are not results and activity counts are not success. Map risky assumptions to cheap discriminating experiments and evidence that would change a decision. Research guidance supplies technical-comparison and evaluation detail when consequential.

End with ordered immediate advances tied to the ultimate demonstration and nearer horizons. Explain each observed gap/opportunity, current capability, incremental outcome, timing, dependencies and continuation evidence. Distinguish preserving, extending, investigating/validating and building; uncertain evidence may warrant validation rather than rebuilding. Choose useful priorities, not a quota or duplicate backlog.

## 5. Review and deliver

**Read `docs/prompts/agents/envision/review.prompt.md` before briefing reviewers or repairing findings.** Give independent reviewers their applicable remit and necessary brief/evidence, not the whole authoring workflow by rote. The author owns revisions; reviews remain read-only and advisory. Use one initial pass, one bounded repair and affected-reviewer verification, then report remaining findings without another automatic repair loop. Unavailable capability means disclosed pending coverage, never impersonated review or host reconfiguration. Keep the compact status honest even when review is omitted.

**Read `docs/prompts/agents/envision/delivery.prompt.md` before final validation, status and adoption handoff.** Deliver one usable argument, accurate reviewed-versus-delivered revision coverage and material open decisions. Explain the direction, value, horizons, ordered immediate advances and evidence limits without starting subsequent work.
