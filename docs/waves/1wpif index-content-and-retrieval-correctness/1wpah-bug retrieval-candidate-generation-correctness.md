# Make Retrieval Candidate Generation Honor Filters and ANN Tuning

Wave: 1wpif index-content-and-retrieval-correctness

## Rationale

Healthy semantic search and lexical fallback retrieve a bounded global candidate window and apply language or per-file constraints afterward. A selected-language result just outside that window becomes an incorrect zero; skewed per-file results underfill even when enough eligible files exist. Separately, production declares Lance `nprobes=20` and `refine_factor=10` but never applies either setting, so runtime recall and latency do not match the documented tuning.

## Requirements

1. Single-language filters SHALL constrain Lance and FTS candidate generation before bounded top-k selection.
2. Category and per-file limits SHALL continue bounded retrieval until the requested result count is filled or an honest safety ceiling is reached. For constraints that cannot be pushed down, each source/table SHALL use monotonic candidate windows `30 → 60 → 120 → 240`, at most four queries and 240 examined rows per source/table per public call; dense and lexical work SHALL expose their individual accounting within the shared public-call budget. For multi-source tools the public-call aggregate SHALL be stated explicitly and tested as an aggregate, not only per source (prepare-council A6, refined by the readiness lanes): the asserted `code_ask` ceiling is 16 substrate queries / 960 examined rows over the four fused candidate sources with the artifact-anchored keyword pass suppressed in the fixture; the keyword pass, when it fires, is accounted as a fifth source (20/1200), and the assessment-class derived docs evidence expansion (one additional docs vector query plus one additional lexical pass) is counted in the formula for assessment calls; definition-boost keyword injections and direct-artifact owner-row reads are declared outside the substrate-query definition (store reads, not searches). The reranker input window is unchanged by refill (refill feeds bounded selection, never the cross-encoder cap) or, if it changes, is itself part of the stated per-call accounting (PERF-RDY-4).
3. Underfill diagnostics SHALL distinguish exhausted eligible results from a bounded-retrieval ceiling. A ceiling exit SHALL return typed `bounded_ceiling_reached` with rounds and examined-row counts; substrate exhaustion SHALL use a distinct reason.
4. Production Lance queries SHALL apply supported ANN tuning explicitly, or remove unsupported constants and document the actual defaults.
5. The inert `LANCEDB_NPROBES`/`LANCEDB_REFINE_FACTOR` constants SHALL be resolved under Requirement 4's retire-and-document branch: production keeps its current (library-default ANN) behavior, the constants are removed, and the actual defaults are documented. No ANN-tuning threshold battery ships in this wave: the prepare council established (2026-08-31, red-team and security seats concurring) that the shipped evaluator has no exact-search reference mode, no ANN-versus-exact overlap metric, and no improvement certifier, that per-query recall floors quantize to 1.0 on fixtures with four or fewer labels, and that new fixtures would invalidate the standing baseline digest. Any future ANN tuning SHALL be measured in wave `1wpih index-quality-evaluation-and-ranking`, where the exact-search reference and overlap metric are built as first-class evaluator features; such claims are baseline-relative and the frozen `1sear` corpus is untouched by this wave.
6. Public tool descriptions and internal comments SHALL match the resulting candidate windows, rerank order, and filter behavior.

## Scope

**Problem statement:** Post-window filtering loses eligible matches, and declared ANN tuning is inert.

**In scope:** Lance/FTS predicates, bounded refill, per-file diversity, honest resolution of the inert ANN constants (retire and document), diagnostic and description consistency, and adversarial tests.

**Out of scope:** Embedding/reranker replacement, unbounded scans, general relevance tuning, golden-suite creation already owned by `1sear`, and the ANN-tuning measurement battery (exact-search reference, overlap metric, improvement certification) — relocated to `1wpih` by the 2026-08-31 prepare council.
