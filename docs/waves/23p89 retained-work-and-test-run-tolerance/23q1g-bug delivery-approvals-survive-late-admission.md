# Delivery approvals survive a late admission

Change Status: `implemented`

Status: planned
Last verified: 2026-11-17

## Rationale

Field report, relayed by the operator on 2026-11-17 from a project using Wavefoundry: a wave had delivery approvals recorded on one date; two changes were admitted afterwards; `wf_review_wave` still showed every delivery lane and the council delivery verdict as approved, so the wave could have closed with no delivery review of the late changes.

A read-only investigation on 2026-11-17 confirmed the mechanism. A delivery approval pins no receipt: `policy_receipt_id` is written only for readiness approvals, and the projection in `review_evidence.review_authority_projection` checks a delivery approval only for actor, independence and finding chronology ("not receipt-bound"). Admitting a change makes the receipt stale until re-Prepare; re-Prepare publishes a receipt with the larger change set, which lapses readiness approvals only.

Storing a receipt id on delivery approvals is not an option: evidence validation rejects that field on anything but a readiness approval, so an earlier framework version would reject a newer ledger during an upgrade. The receipt in force is therefore inferred from the ledger's order, which is append-only.

A second field report the same day said that marking an acceptance criterion or task complete or deferred invalidated the receipt. Executed on scratch copies by the investigation and again by the qa-reviewer lane: ticking a criterion or a task complete, deferring a task, trailing whitespace on the item, a `Change Status` change, a Progress Log row and a `Last verified` stamp do not move the digest. Deferring a criterion rotates the receipt by an earlier decision. Some events move the digest without that being intended; this change does not fix them, and states them:

- Re-ticking a deferred criterion complete with `wf_mark_ac` moves the digest but publishes no receipt, so review is blocked until re-Prepare.
- A note added by hand after a task item's checkbox moves the digest.
- Ticking a checkbox written in a form the normalizer does not reset (inside a blockquote, a `*` or numbered item, a `[-]` mark) moves the digest. The mark tools accept those forms by an earlier decision, because the close gate blocks on them.

The operator's constraint: approvals must not be invalidated incorrectly.

## Product Intent

A reviewer or operator reading a wave's review status can trust that every delivery approval shown as current was recorded when the wave already held every change it holds now. Harmless events during delivery (ticking criteria and tasks, status changes, log rows, removing or closing a change) never lapse an approval, and a closed wave's review status never changes.

## Requirements

1. A delivery approval (a lane approval, `council-delivery` or `operator-signoff`) is not current when the wave's current review-policy receipt lists a change id that is absent from the receipt in force when the approval was recorded. The receipt in force is the last `review_policy_receipt` before the approval in the ledger. Change ids are compared whole, so a change whose id is renamed counts as newly admitted. The status of such an approval names the admitted change.
2. A delivery approval stays current through every other event, including:
   1. A receipt rotation with the same change-id set or a smaller one (`wf_remove_change`, `wf_close_change`), and removing and re-admitting a change the earlier receipt already listed.
   2. A spec edit that changes no change id, a workflow-config change, and a requested-lane change.
   3. Ticking a criterion or a task complete, deferring a task, a `Change Status` change, a Progress Log row, a `Last verified` stamp.
3. When either receipt has no change-set data, or no receipt precedes the approval, the approval stays current. On such a wave, which includes ledgers written before receipts recorded their change set, the reported case is not detected; the changelog says so.
4. A closed wave's review status is unchanged by this change wherever it is rendered or evaluated: the review and close gates, the guided review actions, the `wf_review_event` list, the status table the dashboard shows, the wave resource, lint, the upgrade's status refresh and the test runner's bookkeeping check. Renders that receive the wave record's text read the status from it. Callers that pass only ledger records pass the status explicitly, and the parameter's default applies the rule, so a caller that forgets it shows a difference instead of silently disabling the rule.
5. Recording a delivery approval, including `operator-signoff`, is refused while the wave's receipt is stale. The refusal names re-Prepare and writes nothing. It reuses the staleness recompute the readiness approval path already performs, with the same behaviour when staleness cannot be established for an environmental reason (a warning, not a refusal). A wave with no receipt and no re-prepare marker is not refused. Readiness approvals and findings behave as today: a readiness approval on a stale receipt is already refused, and a finding is accepted. Re-preparing an implementing wave after a plan edit lapses readiness approvals as it does today.
6. This change alters no mark-tool behaviour and no normalization: `gardener_metadata.py` and the review context builder are not edited, no existing spec's digest moves, and the section comparison stays available for receipts recorded before this change. The review-policy evaluator version is not changed.
7. An advisory, never a block, appears as a review diagnostic when the available section data shows that Requirements or Acceptance Criteria text changed between the receipt in force at a delivery approval and the current receipt. When the comparison is unavailable (the normalizer's source differs, or a receipt carries no review context) the advisory says so. It is never written into the persisted status text.
8. The wording of a current approval's status is unchanged, including the phrase "not receipt-bound", so no open wave's persisted status block becomes stale under lint. New wording appears only for an approval that this change makes not current. Keeping the phrase is a deliberate trade-off: it stays accurate in the sense that no receipt id is bound, and the documents of Requirement 9 carry the full rule.
9. The rule is stated where the current one is:
   1. The four tool description sites in `wf_server/server_impl.py` that say receipt rotation lapses readiness approvals only: the two response helpers and the two registered tools for `wf_review_event` and `wf_review_wave`. The `wf_review_event` description also states the refusal of Requirement 5.
   2. `docs/specs/mcp-tool-surface.md`: the passage that says delivery approvals are not receipt-bound, and the passage on finding phase and approval currency.
   3. `docs/architecture/data-and-control-flow.md`: delivery approvals are finding-scoped and membership-scoped, and between an admission and re-Prepare the status still shows them as current while review and close are blocked as stale.
   4. The agent harness core seed's statement that a delivery approval stays current until a later synthesis names its lane.
   5. The review-wave prompt and its shipped template, and the close-wave seed: one sentence each on what a late admission does to delivery approvals.
10. `CHANGELOG.md` Unreleased states: the rule in operator terms; that an open wave with a late admission shows delivery approvals pending after upgrade and how to recover (re-prepare, then record the affected delivery approvals again; regenerate the status block by running review); that a ledger without change-set data is not detected; the refusal of Requirement 5; that closed waves are unchanged; for distributions, that code rendering the review status projection must pass the wave's status; and the unintended digest moves this change leaves in place.

## Scope

**Problem statement:** a change admitted after delivery review inherits the wave's earlier delivery approvals.

**In scope:**

- The projection rule of Requirements 1 to 4 and its consumers.
- The write-time refusal of Requirement 5.
- The advisory of Requirement 7.
- Tests, tool descriptions, the behavior contract, the architecture doc, the seeds, the prompts and the changelog.

**Out of scope:**

- Binding delivery approvals to the receipt, and recording a change-id list on new delivery approvals (see the Decision Log).
- Blocking on a spec edit after delivery approval. The section comparison becomes unavailable whenever the normalizer's source changes, so a block would either fail open or lapse approvals on upgrade.
- What a criterion deferral lapses, and the re-tick of a deferred criterion: `23q1h-bug criterion-deferral-lapses-readiness-approvals`, which is not in this wave.
- Any change to how specs are normalized, and any change to what the mark tools accept.
- Changing which events lapse readiness approvals.
