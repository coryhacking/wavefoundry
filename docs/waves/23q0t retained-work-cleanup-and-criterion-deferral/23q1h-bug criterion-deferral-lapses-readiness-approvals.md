# Criterion deferral lapses readiness approvals

Change ID: `23q1h-bug criterion-deferral-lapses-readiness-approvals`
Change Status: `implemented`
Owner: Engineering
Status: planned
Last verified: 2026-11-17
Wave: 23q0t retained-work-cleanup-and-criterion-deferral

## Rationale

Deferring an acceptance criterion (`[~]`) rotates the wave's review-policy receipt, and every readiness approval lapses: all required lanes and the council readiness verdict must be recorded again in the middle of implementation. A downstream user reported this as the receipt being invalidated by marking a criterion. The behaviour was a deliberate decision in wave `1ui1d` (change `1ug66`): a deferral says a requirement was intentionally not met, which is a scope statement a reviewer must see.

The reviewers who must see it are the ones who judge the delivered scope. A readiness approval was given before implementation on a plan that included the criterion; a narrower plan is a subset of what it approved, and repeating readiness review mid-implementation shows nobody the deferral at the point where it matters. The council delivery verdict and the operator sign-off do judge the delivered scope, and they are stale only if they were recorded before the deferral.

Observed failure, executed on 2026-11-17 against a copy of wave `23p89` through `_prepare_policy_state` (expected: the digest and receipt inputs stay as they were; actual):

1. Deferring a criterion with today's tool form (`[~]` plus ` *reason*`) moves the policy-input digest, requires a new receipt and, because the reason text reached the trigger scan, flips `delivery_council_required` from false to true.
2. Deferring with the mark alone also moves the digest.
3. Re-ticking or reopening the deferred criterion moves the digest again, to a third value, and publishes no receipt, so review is blocked until re-Prepare (the item `23q1g` left in place).

A first design added a deferred-criteria field to the receipt. It failed readiness review: a readiness approval is current only while its pinned receipt id equals the current receipt id, a receipt is appended only when its semantic fields change, and 1.43.0's `validate_policy_receipt` rejects any new receipt field. This design leaves receipts unchanged.

## Product Intent

An implementer can defer a criterion, with its reason, without the wave's readiness approvals lapsing. The deferral stays visible in review as a scope change, and a council delivery verdict or operator sign-off recorded before it must be recorded again before the wave can close.

## Design

Five parts, each executed in a scratch copy before these requirements were written (Progress Log, 2026-11-17 replan rows):

1. The review-policy normalizer treats a criterion's deferred mark and its status note in one canonical form as tracking, as it already treats a complete mark. The criterion's text stays in the digest.
2. `council-delivery` and `operator-signoff` approval records carry, in their existing free-text `limitations` field, a line recording each criterion that was deferred or carried a canonical status note when they were recorded, with a short hash of its note. 1.43.0 accepts this; it rejects any new structured field.
3. The shared Review and Close delivery gate blocks when a criterion deferred or noted now was not deferred then, or its note differs from the recorded hash, and names it. A record without the line stays current.
4. `wf_review_wave` lists every deferred criterion, and every other criterion carrying a canonical status note, as an advisory.
5. `wf_mark_ac` writes the note in the canonical form and only writes the document; it publishes no receipt.

## Requirements

1. **Digest-neutral deferral.** `gardener_metadata.normalize_checkbox_tracking`, applied through `canonical_review_policy_body`, resets an Acceptance Criteria `[~]` mark to `[ ]` as it resets `[x]`. In the Acceptance Criteria section it also removes a status note in the canonical form, whatever the item's mark:
   1. a trailing ` *Status: <reason>*` at the end of an item's first line, where the reason contains no `*` and no line break;
   2. a continuation line that is exactly `*Status: <reason>*` after optional leading indentation, at any blockquote depth, and only when it follows an item's line or that item's other continuation lines; a `*Status: ...*` line that follows no item stays digested.

   Every other byte of a criterion stays digested. Deferring a criterion, reopening it or re-ticking it, by `wf_mark_ac` or by hand, in the mark alone or with a canonical note, leaves the policy-input digest, required lanes, `delivery_council_required` and the council seats unchanged. No receipt is published and no readiness approval lapses.
2. **Text edits keep today's behaviour.** A reworded criterion, a new criterion, a removed criterion, and a deferral that also changes the criterion's text or adds a note in any other form (for example today's ` *reason*`) move the digest; after re-Prepare, readiness approvals lapse as today. Task marks stay neutral as today. The review-policy evaluator version is not changed, because changing it rotates every receipt on upgrade.
3. **`wf_mark_ac`.** Deferring a criterion with a reason writes the first-line note as ` *Status: <reason>*`, replacing today's ` *<reason>*`. The continuation-line form of change `23042` is unchanged. The tool refuses, and writes nothing, a reason that contains `*` or a line break, because the normalizer would not recognise the note and the digest would move with no receipt. A deferral writes only the document, as a completion mark does: no `review_policy_receipt` is appended, the response has no `review_receipt_refreshed` field, and no `review_policy_receipt_superseded` diagnostic is emitted. When the deferral makes a current `council-delivery` or `operator-signoff` approval not current under Requirement 5, the response carries an advisory diagnostic naming each one.
4. **Recorded criterion status.** When a `council-delivery` or `operator-signoff` approval is recorded on a wave with a review-policy receipt, the builder appends one final line to the record's `limitations` text: `Criterion status when recorded: ` followed by one line of JSON serialized with sorted keys and no whitespace between tokens (Python `json.dumps(value, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":"), ensure_ascii=True)`), so equal state gives equal bytes. The object maps each admitted change id to an object, empty when nothing qualifies, that maps the leading AC id of every criterion that at that moment is marked `[~]` or carries a canonical status note (Requirement 1.1 or 1.2) to `{"deferred":<true or false>,"note":"<hash>"}`. `<hash>` is the first 12 lowercase hex characters of SHA-256 over the UTF-8 bytes of the criterion's note text, and the empty string when the criterion has no canonical note. The note text is the reason of each canonical note of that criterion, taken from between `*Status:` and the closing `*` and stripped of surrounding whitespace, joined with one `\n` in document order. The server reads the admitted change documents inside the existing `wf_review_event` transaction under the publication lock, using the same item and note recognition as Requirement 1. The tool alone writes the line: a request whose own `limitations` contains the prefix anywhere is refused and appends nothing, and only the final line of a recorded `limitations` is parsed. Specialist lane approvals and readiness approvals are written as today. No new record field is added. This change is unreleased, so there is exactly one line format and no older form to read.
5. **Delivery rule.** On a wave that is not closed, the shared delivery gate used by `wf_review_wave` and `wf_close_wave` (`lifecycle_gates._evaluate_shared_delivery_state`) emits the blocking diagnostic `delivery_approval_predates_deferral` for the latest `council-delivery` and the latest `operator-signoff` approval when, for a change id the approval's line lists, a criterion with a leading AC id is now marked `[~]` or carries a canonical note, and either:
   1. it is absent from that change's recorded object, which is a deferral or a note added after the approval;
   2. it is marked `[~]` now and was recorded with `"deferred":false`, which is a deferral after the approval that kept an earlier note;
   3. its current note hash differs from the recorded hash, which is a note reworded, added or removed while the criterion stays deferred or noted.

   The message names the signoff key, the change id, the criterion id and which of the three cases applies (deferred, noted, or note changed), and its recovery is to record that approval again with a fresh `context_id`. One code covers the three cases because the recovery is the same. These cases add nothing:
   1. an approval record without the line, or with a line that does not parse, which is read as no information;
   2. a change id the line does not list, which is the late-admission case of change `23q1g`;
   3. a deferred or noted criterion without a leading AC id, which is listed only by Requirement 6;
   4. a closed wave, using `review_evidence.wave_record_closed` (in the gate, `authority.wave_closed`);
   5. a criterion that was recorded but now is neither `[~]` nor noted: its note was removed or it was restored to `[ ]` or `[x]` without a note. This widens the scope back to the plan the readiness approvals judged; it narrows nothing the delivery approval accepted, so it does not block. A criterion restored with its note kept (Scope) has the recorded hash and does not block either. A note removed from a criterion that stays deferred is case 3 and blocks, because the deferral the approval saw has changed its stated reason.

   Specialist delivery lane approvals are not affected. Events that now block: a deferral, a new canonical note on any criterion, or a reworded or removed note on a criterion that stays deferred, each after the approval. Events that do not block, checked against the standing constraint that approvals are not invalidated incorrectly: ticking a criterion complete without a note (it does not qualify for the map), task marks (Tasks is not read), Progress Log rows, `Change Status`, `Last verified` and other metadata (outside Acceptance Criteria), restoring a deferred criterion with or without its note, removing a note from a criterion that is not deferred, and re-deferring with the same note a criterion the approval saw deferred. The status rows, the persisted status block, the dashboard and the `wf_review_event` list are not changed; Decision Log row "Gate, not rows" records this choice and its gap.
6. **Review listing.** On a wave that is not closed, `wf_review_wave` in the implementation phase emits one advisory diagnostic `deferred_criterion_scope_change` per deferred criterion of each admitted change, naming the change id and quoting the item as written, including its reason, whether or not anything lapsed. Because Requirement 1 removes a canonical note from the digest whatever the item's mark, it also emits one advisory `criterion_status_note` per `[ ]` or `[x]` criterion carrying a canonical note, quoting the note, so no digest-neutral text goes unseen at review. Neither is written into the persisted status text.
7. **Reason rule unchanged.** A deferred criterion at required priority still needs its inline reason; `wf_mark_ac` and docs-lint apply `_check_tilde_required_ac_has_inline_note` as today. ` *Status: <reason>*` satisfies it.
8. **Upgrade, rollback and closed waves.**
   1. An open wave whose admitted changes hold a deferred criterion or a canonical status note gets a new digest on upgrade. Review and Close report `review_policy_receipt_stale` until re-Prepare. Re-Prepare publishes a receipt, and readiness approvals pinned to the earlier receipt lapse once. An open wave with neither keeps its digest.
   2. Nothing recomputes the digest of a closed wave. A closed wave's review status, records and renders are unchanged.
   3. Editing `gardener_metadata.py` changes `review_policy.normalizer_signature()`, which is the source hash of that file and not part of receipt identity. Until a new receipt is published, the section comparison of a receipt recorded before the upgrade reports "Incompatible normalization", and a receipt snapshot reports `snapshot_normalizer_mismatch; full review required`. The unreleased tree already moved this signature relative to 1.43.0 (`3537c761...` to `f9531d02...`, from wave `23lxd`'s comment edits), so the next release has this consequence whether or not this change lands.
   4. Under 1.43.0 (rollback or the upgrade's old-code window), an approval record carrying the line validates and projects as today. A receipt this change published for a wave holding a deferral reads as stale and needs one re-Prepare.
9. **Stated rule.** These places stop saying that a deferral refreshes the receipt or that `[~]` deferrals stay significant in the digest, and state Requirements 1, 3, 5 and 8:
   1. the `wf_mark_ac` description and the comment above its receipt refresh in `wf_server/server_impl.py`; one sentence each in the `wf_review_wave` and `wf_review_event` descriptions;
   2. the `normalize_checkbox_tracking` and `canonical_review_policy_body` docstrings;
   3. `docs/specs/mcp-tool-surface.md`: the evaluator-version passage ("contract prose and `[~]` AC deferrals remain significant") and the `wf_mark_ac(state="~")` passage;
   4. `docs/architecture/data-and-control-flow.md`: "This includes publication following automatic deferral";
   5. seed 209: "including receipt publication after a deferral"; seed 170, the section on AC and task checkbox states and the `[~]` marker: the canonical note form and that a deferral lapses no readiness approval;
   6. `CHANGELOG.md` Unreleased: the rule in operator terms, the reversed behaviours, the upgrade cost and recovery of Requirement 8 (re-Prepare, then record readiness approvals again), the 1.43.0 compatibility, and that the re-tick item listed there by `23q1g` as "left in place" is fixed;
   7. `docs/architecture/testing-architecture.md`: the sentence that the runner guard's `canonical_review_policy_body` keeps "AC labels, `[~]`, substantive handoff prose and plan meaning" material;
   8. the rendered `docs/prompts/plan-spec.prompt.md` (the checkbox-states passage restated from seed 170) and `docs/prompts/prepare-wave.prompt.md` (the AC priority check's `[~]` passage), reconciled with seed 170's canonical note form.

**Platforms:** platform-neutral logic, the same on Windows, macOS, Linux and WSL2. `normalize_carriers` runs before the checkbox normalizer, so a CRLF document normalizes as an LF one, and `wf_mark_ac` keeps a document's line endings as today; a CRLF fixture is part of AC-4.

## Reversed shipped behaviour

1. Change `1ug66` (wave `1ui1d`) and its later refinements: "A successful deferred AC also refreshes its review receipt; it never carries an approval forward and returns the fresh review actions." After this change a deferral refreshes no receipt and lapses no readiness approval. Delivery approvals are handled by Requirement 5 instead.
2. The `normalize_checkbox_tracking` rule "An AC `[~]` is deliberately preserved", and the tool reference sentence "contract prose and `[~]` AC deferrals remain significant".
3. `wf_mark_ac`'s first-line reason form ` *<reason>*`, which becomes ` *Status: <reason>*`.
4. The control in change `23q1g`'s neutrality test: "re-ticking a deferred criterion moves the digest". It now must not move.

The search for earlier decisions found nothing else to reverse. Change `21j63`'s note that a `[~]`/`[x]` flip rotates the framework test receipt is about the test receipt, not the review-policy receipt, and is not affected.

## Scope

**Problem statement:** a deferral lapses approvals that did not need to see it and leaves the ones that did untouched.

**In scope:**

- The normalizer change of Requirement 1 and the `wf_mark_ac` change of Requirement 3.
- The recorded set, the gate rule and the review listing of Requirements 4 to 6.
- Tests, tool descriptions, the behavior contract, the architecture doc, seeds 170 and 209, and the changelog.

**Out of scope:**

- Any receipt field, and any change to receipt identity or the evaluator version.
- Changing the status rows, the persisted status block or the dashboard for Requirement 5.
- The late-admission rule and the stale-receipt refusal of `23q1g`, which this change builds on without altering.
- The other unintended digest moves `23q1g` lists: a hand note after a task item, and ticks in forms the normalizer does not reset.
- Removing a status note when a criterion is reticked; the note stays as history and is digest-neutral.
