## Rationale

The operator is trying Envision in real projects after local build 1.39.0+q02v. Tensorwell's author reports that capability validation corrected factual mistakes and reviews improved the roadmap, but personal infrastructure became the recommended destination before other futures received comparable exploration. A per-user deployment became a strategic anchor; broader organizational inference was bundled with a general agent platform and discounted for demand uncertainty that also affected the favored option. The author acknowledges inadequate execution of existing guidance. This is one reported field case, not independently reproduced evidence of universal failure or proof that a larger destination is better.

The operator also finds separate vision, research and review documents burdensome. The desired default is one readable vision and roadmap, with consequential research supporting its argument and review accounting in the response. The current seed defaults review evidence to a sibling and permits a separate research file. The operator also selected phased loading within this wave: a short always-read strategic core, with procedural detail read at its point of use. This tests an attention-management hypothesis; neither shorter text nor file separation alone proves improved adherence.

Solaris has now reported a second field run on 1.39.0+q02v, using Claude Code rather than the Tensorwell run's Codex host. Its author again developed one destination concretely and left others as labels; inferred local-first, lighting-only and ecosystem limits became strategic boundaries. An accurate capability inventory was misrepresented in the draft as missing functionality. All four review follow-ups reportedly found additional issues, followed by author edits to a final revision none had reviewed. Single-document delivery was explicitly requested and mostly worked; it is not evidence for the old default. These reports strengthen the motivation but remain author self-assessments, not independent artifact audits or a controlled cross-host comparison. Teton remains reserved for the next iteration.

## Product Intent

Help product owners discover a valuable, ambitious future and understand how to make it real. Before: the author can favor a polished extrapolation of today's implementation and split its reasoning across files. After: when the destination is open, a reader can explain serious alternative futures and why one is recommended, distinguish initial deployment from chosen product responsibility, and use one coherent document to understand the destination, useful horizons and immediate advances. The author receives a focused strategic core and reads applicable phase guidance before acting, rather than loading every procedural branch at entry. An operator-chosen destination remains authoritative; bigger is not automatically better.

This supports the adopted Wavefoundry vision's [H3 intent-to-work outcome](../../../VISION.md#horizon-3--executable-intent-and-sustained-missions) and [H4 exploration of materially different approaches](../../../VISION.md#horizon-4--competing-solutions-and-coordinated-engineering). It improves authoring foundations, not autonomous execution or those horizons' completion. The opening, roadmap overview and relevant horizon passages were consulted; they introduce no prerequisite authorizing broader runtime work. AC-2 and AC-3 test the user outcome; AC-1/4/5 test instruction delivery, strategic review and compatibility.

## Requirements

1. **Distinguish origin, starting point and possible destination.** Separate the problem that started the project, operator-established commitments, current implementation/deployment choices, deferred capabilities and proposed changes of product responsibility. Explicit current requirements remain binding until changed; neither dismiss them as accidents nor silently make them permanent limits. Name obligations introduced by a scope expansion and expose consequential choices to the operator. The first beneficiary or deployment need not define eventual reach.
2. **Explore fairly before recommending an open destination.** Before drafting the recommended destination, develop each serious alternative through a comparably concrete beneficiary experience, distinctive value, consequential obligations and uncertainties. This is an authoring sequence, not a requirement to put alternatives before the summary in the delivered vision. Compare product futures, not only engines or implementation methods. Explain the recommendation and sacrifices; apply missing and contrary evidence consistently to the favored option too. Comparable attention does not mean equal length, exhaustive research, a fixed option count or an invented optimum. Do not manufacture strategic alternatives when the operator has chosen the destination. Where ambition is requested, seriously examine transformative capacity beyond the backlog without imposing enterprise, cloud, autonomous-agent or platform scope. Do not assume commercialization is the objective: personal usefulness, open contribution, adoption, financial return and other value can support different futures. Distinguish a product purpose from a distribution route or business model; options should differ in beneficiary experience or responsibility, not merely packaging.
3. **Make consequential inference visible without new ceremony.** Place inferred beneficiary/scope/destination choices near the recommendation, with their implications and unresolved decisions. Silence is not selection. Continue legitimate provisional work; use the existing combined briefing for genuine blockers. Do not require the operator to choose a market before Envision helps explore it. Reuse concrete operator or beneficiary experience where available; ask for a decision-relevant missing example rather than inventing daily use or adding a mandatory interview. Personal usefulness, open adoption and income may coexist; a hobby or small team constrains pace without automatically capping the destination. Treat an interesting reviewer hypothesis as a hypothesis until supported or selected. Preserve useful horizons, technical and nontechnical dependencies, intermediate value, endurance, adaptability and lower precision at distance.
4. **One coherent vision by default.** Write the existing canonical vision or root VISION.md; keep full review accounting in the final response. Retain a compact one- or two-line status in the vision naming the review date, reviewed revision or revisions, roles actually run, and pending finding count and unreviewed changes (or explicitly unknown/not reviewed). The status must distinguish the delivered content from the reviewed content without implying adoption or approval of subsequent edits. Integrate decision-relevant research conclusions, citations, enduring assumptions, contrary evidence and open strategic choices into the argument. A concise appendix is optional when useful. Do not concatenate research notes and review transcripts into a giant document or bury all evidence in an appendix. Consolidate general authorization/adoption/evidence caveats while retaining specific limits where a decision depends on them. A concise account of the operative brief belongs in the argument where it explains consequential choices; preserve the operator's meaning without requiring a verbatim interview transcript. Research limitations that affect those choices and the compact review status remain in the vision; full reviewer chronology and detailed revision coverage belong in the delivery response. No word ceiling on visions.
5. **Preserve artifacts and explicit exceptions.** An explicit project/operator evidence-location instruction overrides the new default. Substantial separately reusable research may justify a companion: state its concrete purpose before creating it and honor a single-document request. Existing vision-output siblings do not automatically authorize new output files, deletion, overwrite or silent invalidation. On an authorized revision, incorporate needed conclusions, preserve working references and report historical siblings left unchanged. Retirement or relocation requires operator direction and preservation of unique evidence/links. Read-only requests write nothing. The current change does not consolidate real-project vision files.
6. **Sharpen existing strategic-review remits.** Product strategy and independent challenge examine uneven alternatives, asymmetric uncertainty, conflated scopes and deployment assumptions promoted to destiny. Assess recommendation quality and connection of immediate advances to the larger future. Preserve current stakes-based coverage, independence, bounded repair and affected-reviewer verification. Report consequential contributions honestly: a no-new-finding follow-up is verification, not proof of uselessness, convergence or efficacy. Give verification reviewers the identified revision, prior findings and actual changes as claims to verify, not predetermined accepted or resolved verdicts. Shared author framing and model limitations remain disclosed. Add no chair, mandatory Council, survey, telemetry or recurring assessment to ordinary Envision runs.
7. **Preserve working contracts.** Retain current-capability classification and validation, attributed operator experience, authorized research and source distinctions, ambition protection, adoption ownership, unrelated-edit preservation, capability-limit reporting and artifact-only authority. No automatic implementation, lifecycle records, adoption, network expansion or environment changes. Carry verified capability classifications through synthesis into priorities; change an established classification only when new evidence supports it. Resolve consequential disagreements among inventory, draft, source and reviewer claims with targeted checks or explicit uncertainty, not another mandatory broad audit. Keep destination and credible roadmap core; avoid option worksheets and mandatory interviews.
8. **Deliver consistently to new and existing targets.** Update seed 179, the local prompt, architecture prose and changelog. Extend and exercise seed160's changed-Envision reconciliation against an old canonical prompt with compatible additions and an explicit local artifact-policy conflict. Preserve additions; surface conflict without overwrite. Upgrade must not run Envision or alter vision/evidence files. Keep the skill a thin pointer; do not duplicate the workflow in accurate catalogs/registry/generated skills. Replace or consolidate instructions rather than accumulate clauses. R10 sets the always-read core target; retain a roughly net-neutral target across the complete canonical guidance set, with no hard total ceiling. Report core, each companion, total guidance and corresponding local counts using the same method, and explain any growth, value retained and redundant material removed. Reviewers assess readability and retained contracts alongside behavioral evidence, without claiming unobserved improvement.
9. **Make the verification boundary and final revision coverage explicit.** After the normal initial review, one bounded author repair and affected-reviewer verification, stop further author repairs and report remaining actionable findings/decisions under the existing stopping rule. Do not silently apply another round and present the result as reviewed. If later operator direction authorizes further edits, identify the new revision and which earlier findings or checks still support it; disclose unverified changes and any focused follow-up actually performed. A usable provisional document may have pending findings. This is a clarification of the existing bounded workflow, not a new approval gate or automatic review loop. Keep R4's compact status accurate at delivery, including after any explicitly authorized later edit. In the delivery response distinguish the final document from the versions each reviewer assessed; a selected role or earlier approval does not cover subsequent changes by implication.


10. **Load procedural guidance at its point of use.** Restructure seed179 and the local Envision prompt as an always-read core targeting approximately 2,000 words or fewer, with four internal phase companions: research/evidence, revision/preservation, advisory review, and delivery/adoption. This is a meaningful reduction of the entry surface, not a numeric ceiling on visions or the total guidance set; explain any deviation from the core target for reviewer judgment. Keep purpose, authority/read-only/adoption limits, output default, research authorization, preservation obligations, commitment-versus-deployment grounding, capability status, alternatives-before-recommendation and roadmap shape in the core. Put their detailed procedures in the relevant phase owner rather than duplicating them. At each applicable step, the core names the exact full project-relative local companion path and requires reading it before the dependent action: research before gathering/assessing evidence, revision before revising an existing vision, review before briefing reviewers or repairing findings, delivery before final validation/status/adoption handoff. Do not eagerly load all companions; retain needed context and reread only when materially changed or unavailable. Core invariants apply even before a phase is loaded; a skipped/omitted review still has honest delivery coverage. Read-only work obeys the same authority boundary. Missing, unreadable or contradictory required guidance stops that dependent step with a concrete report; do not silently skip it, fabricate review coverage, fall back around project policy or run setup/upgrade automatically. Independent reviewers receive their applicable remit and necessary brief/evidence, not a second whole authoring workflow by rote.
11. **Install and reconcile the complete guidance set.** Canonical companions live in `.wavefoundry/framework/seeds/envision/{research,revision,review,delivery}.prompt.md`; project-owned counterparts live in `docs/prompts/agents/envision/` with matching names. Seed100 authors missing companions and core references without exposing helpers as public commands. Seed160 and the local upgrade prompt reconcile the core and companions as one instruction set, using retained old/new canonical text and project additions. Process added, modified or removed members of the explicitly named core/companion source set even when seed179 is unchanged; backfill missing companions through the existing missing-only path. Inspect instruction-set completeness on partial-install/retry rather than treating an unavailable prior diff as proof that nothing needs reconciliation. Preserve compatible customizations in moved clauses, including moving them into the corresponding local companion when safe. Stop affected conflicting or ambiguous merges explicitly; partial reconciliation is not completion, and old inline rules must not remain as a competing owner after a successful move. Repetition is stable. Packaging and extraction must deliver all companions using existing mechanisms; no new loader, registry entry or public command is introduced. Upgrade neither runs Envision nor changes actual vision/review/research outputs. Local guidance companions are operating instructions, not the output siblings R4 removes by default.
