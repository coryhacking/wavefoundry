## Rationale

The distributed Envision workflow has a strong honesty and authority contract, but its authoring guidance needs to lead with the product vision itself. The operator identified ten improvement areas and clarified that this is broader than selecting an investment: establish what the product could become, how it could create the greatest value financially and otherwise, how to reach that destination through steps that deliver value, and how to build something enduring and adaptable.

The current prompt includes horizons, intermediate value and a next investment, but its coverage does not establish that a reader receives a compelling, valuable direction and a convincing path. Whether the current guidance systematically reduces ambition is an unverified behavioral hypothesis, not a proven result. Wave 21u1e tested distribution and a representative vision-authoring execution. Its Archetype Council reviewed the Pantry Map vision, not the Envision prompt; those are different review targets. The later strategy review identifies a second evidence boundary: repository grounding establishes current capability, not customer need or market opportunity. The follow-up brings that missing diagnosis and discovery discipline into the authoring contract.

## Product Intent

Help product owners and builders imagine an ambitious, distinctive and consequential future for their product: where could it go, what could become possible for people, and why would that matter? Ground the destination in who benefits and how, without limiting it to an extrapolation of today's implementation or backlog. Connect that destination to technical foundations and a sequence of demonstrable advances that create value along the way, endure in operation and adapt as needs and evidence change. Financial return, productivity and other relevant forms of value are considered without assuming one universal measure or business model. The vision must connect a sourced or explicitly hypothesized diagnosis to customer value, strategic choices and a plausible route; the workflow structures discovery but cannot substitute for primary research or stakeholder alignment. Selecting the next bounded investment follows from this argument; it is not the purpose of the whole document. AC-2 and AC-3 test representative reader understanding and ambition preservation; delivery of the updated guidance and discovery wording is owned by companion change 21vpo; they do not prove future commercial success, universal authoring quality or achieved product outcomes.

## Requirements

1. Build an ambitious product vision on a compact diagnosis: the beneficiary's problem or desired progress, what makes it hard, the pivotal insight and why this opportunity is timely. Source the enabling change where known; an enduring need or an unproven timing hypothesis is acceptable, invented urgency is not. When the destination is open, explore meaningfully different futures and recommend one; when it is chosen, preserve it. Express a memorable destination through a concrete future experience in the beneficiary's language. The reader should understand why this future matters, why this product could deliver it, the choices it requires and how useful stages make it real. Give reviewers that same outcome test. Vision names the desired future; supporting strategy explains the choices and path. Do not substitute a feature wishlist or framework headings for an argument.
2. Establish the brief through an adaptive strategic interview, reusing known answers. Use this question bank only for material gaps: who struggles and what progress they seek; what they do/use today; what insight the team sees; why now; what and whom the product will deliberately not serve; who adopts, pays for or sustains it and why; what failure would look like even if it ships; and the desired ambition. Use one consolidated pre-draft exchange only when material information is missing: combine the necessary discovery questions, inferred-brief readback and review-scope notice. If the brief is sufficient, state the interpretation and proceed without asking for confirmation. Defer nonblocking questions into the draft as open decisions. Do not ask separately to approve the readback or routine review scope; a genuinely blocking strategic ambiguity may require clarification, with its consequence explained. Ground needs in supplied interviews, observations, usage/support evidence or research as well as repository facts. Label sourced observations, interpretations and untested hypotheses distinctly; a codebase or internal conviction does not establish demand. Name the cheapest useful research for the most consequential uncertain need, without claiming a fixed interview count validates it. Missing primary research permits a provisional vision with visible gaps, not fabricated customers or implied validation. Consider financial and nonfinancial value, costs and beneficiary tradeoffs without forcing a business model or invented common score.
3. Protect the requested ambition across every review and repair. Challenges to the size or direction of the destination remain explicit strategic decisions for the operator. Correct false statements about today's capabilities, broken reasoning, unsupported certainty and dependency errors; do not silently replace the destination with a safer or smaller one. Evidence that a destination may be infeasible must remain visible, with consequences and alternatives, rather than being suppressed in the name of ambition. Distinguish desired outcomes, assumptions and established facts.
4. Work backward from the ultimate experience to necessary capabilities and foundations, then forward through horizons that deliver useful outcomes. Every horizon needs a demonstration, exit criteria, a failure condition and intermediate value; cover other elements where they explain the path. Assess value, usability, feasibility and viability at the destination, then identify the consequential risk each horizon reduces first and the evidence needed. Viability includes funding/cost to serve, adoption/distribution and operating constraints appropriate to the product, not only revenue. Prefer useful early slices. Where research must precede a usable capability, name the uncertainty, stopping condition and beneficiary value it enables; activity or infrastructure completion alone is not product value. Explain dependencies, parallel work and timing assumptions; more workers do not imply proportional progress. An unchanged baseline must fail a new capability demonstration. Keep detailed tickets outside the vision.
5. Make strategic choices and two kinds of endurance explicit. Compare the intended experience with relevant competing products, the status quo, do-it-yourself and doing nothing. State who/where to focus, why people would choose this product, deliberate non-goals and sacrifices, and why its value or advantage could persist. Distinguish a benefit from a mechanism that sustains it; label unproven advantage claims and do not invent a moat. For public/internal/open products, sustained usefulness, adoption and stewardship can matter more than excluding competitors. Separately explain technical/operational endurance: stable principles, truth/ownership/contracts, maintenance, feedback and human decisions, costly-to-reverse choices, replaceable boundaries and migration. If a major assumption fails, explain what remains useful, what changes and what decision returns to the operator. Preserve justified options without speculative extensibility. Use a supplied landscape or external research within the operator's existing authorized scope; otherwise request the needed research scope or disclose the landscape gap. Do not silently add network research to a local-only engagement.
6. Tie evaluation to beneficiary outcomes. Propose a primary outcome metric with guardrails against foreseeable harm or gaming; use a small justified set if one number would distort the purpose. Explain measurement and observation windows, baselines and limits; separate targets and proposed metrics from observed results. Use independent judgment, repeated attempts and held-out cases where meaningful, and state what cannot yet be justified, simulated, measured or guaranteed. Map the riskiest assumptions to a cheap discriminating experiment and evidence that would change the decision. Revisit when horizons meet/miss exit criteria, critical assumptions or questions change, or new beneficiary/market evidence changes the brief. Optionally test the destination with a short fictional future announcement and three hard customer FAQ questions; keep it temporary unless requested, clearly label it as an imagined future and never invent real testimony. This check tests narrative clarity, not customer validation.
7. Preserve valid existing material and unrelated operator edits when revising a vision. Explain substantive directional changes, reconcile contradictions and list what changed in direction and why in the response. Report how the result meets the brief and what the operator should inspect first. Use only the target project's brief and sources; do not reuse another project's vision or refer to this author's personal-skill history in generic instructions.
8. Keep the enduring vision separate from review-session evidence. Default detailed review findings, actual coverage, dispositions and open review decisions to a sibling such as VISION-review.md, honoring an existing canonical review location or an operator request for response-only evidence. Keep at most a short dated review-status link in the vision; enduring assumptions and strategic open questions still belong in its argument. Preserve existing sibling content rather than overwriting it blindly. Read-only requests write neither artifact and retain the review record in the response.
9. Define substantial revision as a change to purpose, beneficiary, the set of horizons or their order; use affected reviews proportionately for other edits. State the expected full-review scope and effort in that same initial brief/readback; this is a notice with an opportunity to steer, not a second confirmation question. Do not wait again before review when the established scope still applies; return to the operator only for a material scope/authority change or unresolved strategic choice. Under the default five-axis composition, distinguish the eight role contexts (architecture, red-team, chair and five seats) from any actual concurrency or host reuse; do not invent monetary or duration estimates. Reuse already explicit review scope without another approval gate. Preserve independent execution, honest unavailable-review reporting, artifact-only authority and the existing bounded review/repair process.
10. Put the product destination, value, path and enduring/adaptable foundations before detailed review orchestration. Use the depth needed for a coherent, usable workflow; there is no inherited word or line ceiling. Review length by whether each passage contributes distinct guidance, whether an author can follow the priority/order, and whether the important reasoning survives in actual outputs. Report before/after whitespace-separated word counts for seed 179 and the complete local prompt, with one sentence explaining what any growth buys; counts are visibility, not a limit. Reduce review protocols to concise canonical pointers while keeping essential honesty, authority, capability-limit and bounded-repair rules explicit. Remove repetition and procedural duplication, but do not compress away strategic substance to match the previous file's size. Additional structure must improve navigation rather than conceal essential instructions. Keep seed 179's workflow and the self-hosted prompt aligned, allowing only their existing wrapper/metadata differences. Review the revised prompt itself with Archetype Council as an advisory artifact; separately evaluate visions produced from it. Neither activity replaces the follow-up wave's required readiness/delivery reviews or retroactively changes the evidence for 21u1e.

## Evaluation Coverage and Stopping Rules

**Judging independence.** A separate rubric agent writes the judging rubric, fixture packet, pressure challenge, known-bad control and acceptable destination space before the prompt is revised. It works from the Product Intent and the operator's stated findings, not from Requirements 1–10 wording or any prompt draft, and the rubric asks whether the reader can reconstruct the argument, not whether named sections exist. The implementer who revises the prompt does not author or see the rubric, traps or control before the trials run. Readers see neither prompt nor this plan's requirements. Prefer a reader from a different model or context lineage than the authors where available, and disclose the actual lineage either way.

Begin with a matched baseline/candidate ambition comparison, one candidate vision through the actual review ensemble and the combined read-only/revision scenario. These are starting coverage, not a maximum number of trials. Use independent authors and a fresh reader comparing the neutrally labelled outputs first and only then inspecting other scenario evidence. Pair verdicts are recorded before other material is disclosed; later scenario judgments do not silently revise them. Freeze the ambition brief, acceptable destination space and pressure challenge before seeing either output. The brief names beneficiaries, desired scale of impact and constraints while leaving the destination open enough to require synthesis. Include a small dated synthetic customer-evidence packet, one competing alternative/status quo, and at least one plausible but unsupported need or advantage assertion; label all fixture material synthetic and do not present it as real research. The same packet is supplied to both authors. The reader checks whether the candidate uses the evidence, keeps assumptions visible and addresses the alternative instead of ignoring it. Cases may cover multiple ACs when the observations actually establish them; reuse is an efficiency, not a restriction on necessary evidence. The challenge includes a legitimate source-grounded concern and pressure to settle for a smaller outcome; distinguish that pressure from binding operator instructions. Keep the runs isolated; do not feed the baseline answer to the candidate author. Label supplied test challenges as fixture inputs, not as actual independent reviewer findings.

The coordinator explicitly narrows the paired fixture runs to the stated evaluation scope; they do not each launch the full Envision review ensemble. The one ensemble run exists because reviewer-induced downsizing is the motivating hypothesis and a scripted challenge cannot test it. This qualifies authoring and response behavior under controlled challenge, not universal reviewer execution. Actual prompt artifact review uses Archetype Council's canonical chair/isolated-seat protocol and bounded reconciliation; architecture and standalone red-team are not automatically added to that artifact review merely because Envision offers them for visions. Required lifecycle reviews remain separate. Choose additional or held-out cases for the important ways the workflow could fail: for example, evidence-poor versus evidence-rich briefs, commercial versus internal/public viability, or new versus established products. Select contrasting cases for the actual claims, not an exhaustive cross-product. Include a held-out transfer check before claiming transfer across contexts. If variability or disagreement is material, propose an additional batch and its judging criteria under the operator-return rule below; do not stop at the first favorable output or discard failures. Report failed attempts and affected reevaluation after repair.

Before each evaluation batch, record its question, cases, reviewer independence, expected observations and finite stopping point. Conclude when required behaviors have direct evidence, material observed failures are resolved and independently checked, and remaining uncertainty is explicitly outside the supported claim. Delivery QA assesses whether the evidence supports the Product Intent and ACs, rather than treating run count as proof. When a predeclared batch reaches its stopping point and a required outcome remains unproven, or a repair fails its re-check, return the evidence and options to the operator before expanding scope or starting another batch. This includes vision-quality shortfalls, not only access/research blockers. The coordinator may recommend a bounded repair/evaluation, narrowing a claim or stopping, but cannot authorize continuation itself. Preserve unchecked ACs until actual evidence or an explicit documented scope decision resolves them. Explicit prior authorization for a defined multi-stage batch covers only its stated scope and stopping point, never an indefinite loop. Repeated unchanged full framework suites are not an evaluation method for vision quality.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Investment language crowds out product purpose | Lead with destination, beneficiaries and value; treat next step as the resulting decision. |
| Greatest-value ambition becomes an unsupported optimality claim | Compare plausible alternatives, expose value tradeoffs and label uncertain benefits. |
| Review quietly substitutes a modest destination | Preserve ambition, record strategic alternatives for the operator and test narrowing pressure. |
| Endurance becomes overengineering or refusal to evolve | Tie stable foundations and changeable boundaries to plausible needs and evidence; defer unjustified abstractions. |
| Additional guidance increases ceremony | Rebalance existing prose, scale detail to the argument and disclose full review scope before launching it. |
| Small fixture success is overstated | Preserve actual outputs and independent judgments, including failures and sample limits. |
| Repository facts or a persuasive story masquerade as customer evidence | Separate sourced need, interpretation and hypothesis; name missing research and never invent testimony or market proof. |
| Strategy tools dilute the ambitious destination or make the workflow hard to use | Assess depth, navigation and observed usefulness; remove duplication rather than impose an arbitrary word limit or forced framework template. |
| Companion delivery integrates an earlier candidate | Hand off a frozen prompt identity and notify the delivery owner of relevant changes; limit rechecks to affected integration assertions. |
| Evaluation repeatedly expands after a quality shortfall | Stop at the declared boundary and return evidence/options to the operator before another batch. |

## Design References

Primary sources checked on 2026-10-28. These inform concise authoring guidance; they do not establish that a prompt reproduces a consulting engagement or that every framework applies to every product.

- Richard Rumelt, [The perils of bad strategy](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-perils-of-bad-strategy): diagnose the pivotal challenge, choose an approach and coordinate action. Used for the diagnosis-to-path argument, not as a claim that ambition is undesirable.
- Christensen Institute, [Jobs to Be Done](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/): understand the progress people seek in their circumstances, including functional, social and emotional dimensions. Supports beneficiary-language discovery, not inferred demand from code.
- Amazon, [Product management at Amazon](https://aws.amazon.com/executive-insights/content/product-management-at-amazon/): start from customer knowledge and use a future press release/FAQ to make the proposed experience concrete. Our shorter optional check is an adaptation, not a claim of running Amazon's full process.
- Roger Martin, [Five questions to build a strategy](https://www-2.rotman.utoronto.ca/rogermartin/HBR_May26.pdf): connect aspirations, where to play/how to win, capabilities and supporting systems through iteration. Supports explicit choices and capability coherence.
- Marty Cagan, [The Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/): value, usability, feasibility and business viability; address material risks early. Adapt viability to the target's funding and operating model.
- Hamilton Helmer, [7 Powers](https://7powers.com/): strategic durability concerns persistent differential returns, distinct from maintainable architecture. The author's indexed book-site summary was available; direct page fetch returned 403. Do not treat that limited source check as detailed verification of all seven mechanisms.

Outcome guardrails, provisional evidence labels and the bounded trial adaptations above are this plan's design choices. No single framework is treated as a universal checklist. “Why now” is examined without requiring a fabricated market shift, and no one metric is allowed to erase material beneficiary tradeoffs.
