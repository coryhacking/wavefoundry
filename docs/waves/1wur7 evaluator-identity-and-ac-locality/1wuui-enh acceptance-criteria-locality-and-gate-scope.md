## Rationale

Several hundred change documents in this repository carry an acceptance criterion asserting whole-repository state, most commonly "the full framework test suite passes". The clause is not in any seed or template; it spread by copying. It is the wrong shape for an acceptance criterion, because it asserts a property of the whole repository at a moment in time rather than a property of the change.

No total is authoritative, and this document deliberately does not assert one. Three readiness lanes ran independent censuses and none reproduced another's figure: scans returned 11, 74, 156, 221, 317, 329, and 402 depending on the phrasing family and on whether the unit was a file, an AC bullet, or a line. An earlier draft asserted "297" as load-bearing rationale; that number counted files containing the phrase anywhere rather than acceptance criteria, and a first correction to "317" did not reproduce either. Both are withdrawn rather than defended, because a change whose thesis is measurement honesty cannot rest on a statistic that moves with the predicate. Any figure quoted downstream MUST carry its scan predicate.

The load-bearing figure is not the total at all: it is the small set of carriers in NON-closed waves, the only documents any validator can reach. Enumerated at readiness under the broad family Requirement 3 specifies, that set is seven ACs across three waves (`1wpif`: `1wngv` AC-7, `1wpag` AC-6, `1wpah` AC-6; `1wpig`: `1wpai` AC-6, `1wpaj` AC-5; `1wpih`: `1wpid` AC-5, `1wpie` AC-6) plus the ACs inside this wave, with a further four to eight parked under `docs/plans/` depending on predicate.

The consequence is that AC completion becomes non-local and time-dependent. Wave `1wpif` demonstrated both failure modes at once on 2026-08-31:

- `1wngv` AC-7 carries the clause and is marked `[x]`, purely because the suite happened to be green when that lane finished.
- `1wpag` AC-6 and `1wpah` AC-6 carry the same clause and cannot be marked, because a CONCURRENT wave (`1wpig`) has an uncommitted change document in the working tree that trips a repo-wide review-policy test. No code in `1wpif` is implicated; its own suites are green at 7,889 tests.

Three sibling documents in one wave, one clause, opposite outcomes decided by timing and by another wave's in-flight edit. That is the tell: the clause measures the tree, not the work. It also creates a standing temptation at close time to reword the AC so it passes, which is precisely the gate-shaping the house rules forbid.

Repository-state assertions belong at the close gate, which already judges tree health (docs lint, gardener, secrets scan). Per-change acceptance criteria should assert what the change owns.

## Requirements

1. Seed `170-plan-feature.prompt.md` SHALL teach AC locality: an acceptance criterion asserts an outcome the change controls and that a reviewer can verify from the change's own evidence; repository-wide or environment state (whole-suite green, machine load, another wave's artifacts) is a gate concern and SHALL NOT be written as an AC. The existing "name concrete verification evidence" guidance is adjacent and SHALL be extended rather than replaced.
2. The seed SHALL give the replacement shape, so the intent is not lost: the change's own suites and any test it adds pass, the documents the change authors or edits validate, and no failure anywhere is attributable to the change. The shape SHALL NOT say "docs validation passes" unqualified (docs-contract lane, DOCS-RDY-7): full `wf_validate_docs` scans all of `docs/` and can be red because a concurrent wave's in-flight document is malformed, which is the same non-local failure this change exists to eliminate, and the seed would propagate it into every future change document in every target repository.
3. A docs-lint validator SHALL flag an acceptance criterion that asserts repository-wide state, alongside the existing AC validators (`_check_checkbox_ac_syntax`, `_check_ac_priority_alignment`, `_check_tilde_required_ac_has_inline_note`). It SHALL key on the ASSERTION SHAPE, not on a phrase list, and the carve-out for quoting, negating, or specifying the clause SHALL be scoped to the individual AC BULLET, never to the whole document (docs-contract lane, DOCS-RDY-9): a document-level exemption would let any change doc that merely mentions the clause in prose disable the validator for all of its ACs. The rule SHALL name the phrasing families it catches, including the runner-command form and a repo-wide clause compounded into an otherwise change-scoped AC. Its call site SHALL be named: today the AC validators run only inside the wave-owned change-doc loop, so `docs/plans/*.md` is never AC-linted and an author gets no signal until admission, while adding a plans pass would immediately flag the parked plans that carry the clause. Feedforward guidance alone did not prevent the spread; the sensor is the durable half.
4. The validator SHALL NOT retroactively fail existing documents, and the scoping mechanism is the wave record's `Status`, NOT authorship time. The mechanism is SELECTED (docs-contract lane, DOCS-RDY-6) as `_wave_requires_wave_owned_change_docs`, which admits only `{active, implementing, ready}` or an explicit `Activated at:` marker; `docs_constants_validators` is a DIFFERENT precedent that excludes only `closed`, and the two disagree on `planned` and `paused`. Under the selected mechanism no document in `1wpif` (paused), `1wpig` (planned), `1wpih` (planned), or this wave is in scope today, and a carrier enters scope only when its wave is readied or activated. "Authored or modified after the change lands" is REJECTED as inexpressible (readiness QA-RDY-1, CODE-RDY-2): validators receive `(text, rel)` with no git state or mtime, docs-lint's only git signal is the transient working-tree changed set used by the advisory incremental pass rather than the authoritative gate, and `Last verified:` is restamped by the gardener on any changed document, so a legacy file would be pulled into scope by gardening alone. Status scoping covers the overwhelming majority of carriers for free; the residual live carriers SHALL be enumerated with a disposition each.
5. DECIDED (operator, 2026-08-31), SCOPED (docs-contract lane, DOCS-RDY-1): the check applies ONLY where `run_tests.py` is present, which is the framework source repository. `build_pack.py` excludes `scripts/run_tests.py`, `scripts/tests`, and `test-cache.json` from the distribution under the standing policy that "seeds must not instruct them to run tests", so a target repository that vendors the pack can never write the receipt and has no recovery path. An unconditional missing-receipt block would therefore hard-block wave close in every target repository, permanently. Where the runner is absent the check SHALL be a documented no-op that neither blocks nor claims proof, and the hash computation SHALL be reused from `run_tests.py` only when that module exists rather than imported unconditionally. The wave close gate SHALL verify the EXISTING test receipt rather than running a suite or inventing a new artifact. `run_tests.py` already writes `.wavefoundry/framework/test-cache.json` with `inputs_hash`, `ran_at`, `test_count`, and `result`, where `inputs_hash` covers every file under `.wavefoundry/framework/` except packaging artifacts and the cache file itself, so the receipt is self-invalidating the moment any framework file changes. Close SHALL read it and require `result == "ok"` with an `inputs_hash` matching the current framework tree; a missing, red, or stale receipt SHALL be reported as "not proven" rather than assumed green, since `_write_cache` only ever records successful runs. The check SHALL NOT run the suite (no subprocess in the gate path, no wall-clock cost at close).
6. AMENDED at delivery review (QA-DEL-10, operator-approved 2026-09-01). The gate's scope SHALL be stated as it BEHAVES, in both halves: the receipt's `inputs_hash` covers `.wavefoundry/framework/` only, so only receipt STALENESS is framework-scoped, but `run_tests.py` writes a receipt only on a WHOLE-suite pass, so a failure triggered by content under `docs/` leaves no receipt and DOES block close. A green receipt attests the framework code, not the tree. The gate SHALL be described as neither a whole-repository guarantee nor a whole-repository exemption. The original wording below said documentation-triggered breakage "remains a lane-review concern", which is what the gate documents but not what it does. Original wording retained for the record: the receipt's hash covers `.wavefoundry/framework/` only, so it proves the framework code is green and says nothing about a failure triggered by content under `docs/`. Documentation-triggered breakage (the class that blocked wave `1wpif`'s two AC-6 clauses) remains a lane-review concern, and the gate SHALL NOT be described as a whole-repository guarantee.

## Scope

**Problem statement:** an acceptance criterion that asserts whole-repository state cannot be satisfied or falsified by the change it belongs to.

**In scope:**

- Seed `170-plan-feature.prompt.md` AC-authoring guidance; a new AC-shape validator in `wave_lint_lib/wave_validators.py` with tests; the recorded close-gate decision; contributor documentation that repeats the AC contract.

**Out of scope:**

- Rewriting acceptance criteria in existing change documents, closed or open (each wave owns its own; retro-editing closed records is prohibited).
- Any change to the standing retrieval gate or its thresholds.
- Whether the framework suite itself should be faster.
