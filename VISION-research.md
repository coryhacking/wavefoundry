# Wavefoundry: product landscape and evidence

Owner: Engineering
Status: research
Last verified: 2026-10-30

## Decision this research informs

How could Wavefoundry enable a small team to build, take over and continually improve significant software systems, including enterprise maintenance, refactoring, debt reduction and modernization?

**Recommendation:** build toward the autonomous engineering laboratory: department-scale capacity for one engineer or a small team, including creation and continuing enterprise stewardship. Enter through a small team inheriting an important system with fragmented knowledge and a consequential change. Lead with trustworthy acceptance and task-relevant understanding, qualify execution through existing hosts, and recruit a willing design partner alongside measured operating improvements. Test useful engineering evidence as an enterprise asset; audit readiness remains unqualified. Preserve useful continuity and assurance. Execution ownership must earn its place through incremental benefit over integrating existing tools; it is a means, not the destination. The research informs this path but has neither proved nor disproved the ultimate ambition.

This is a strategic inference, not a demonstrated market gap. The landscape already includes broad engineering agents, migration platforms, persistent sessions, code intelligence and reusable procedures. Neither “beyond coding” nor “vendor-neutral agents” is a sufficient differentiation claim. The potential distinction is a maintained, project-owned connection between purpose, obligations, decisions, changes and later outcomes that remains useful across contributors and execution providers. We have not established that buyers want another product to supply it.

This public-source desk research supports [the proposed vision](VISION.md). Initial sources were checked on 2026-10-29; dated additions, including the review products and 8090 Software Factory below, were checked on 2026-10-30. It is an illustrative landscape, not an exhaustive procurement comparison or hands-on qualification. Vendor documentation supports capability descriptions, not effectiveness claims. Controlled studies, observational studies, operator case reports and practitioner guidance are distinguished below. No customers were interviewed, products trialed or confidential sources accessed. Public evidence can guide experiments; it cannot settle willingness to pay, adoption friction or superiority for Wavefoundry.

Published success stories and their reported failure episodes do not supply the denominator of unsuccessful or abandoned projects. Treat them as examples of mechanisms and prerequisites, not representative success rates; publication and selection bias limit transfer to an ordinary enterprise team.

### What the review-products and 8090 comparison changes

Added 2026-10-30. **Trustworthy acceptance can be a necessary engineering foundation without being the strongest standalone market entry.** The rows above establish substantial overlap with both assurance and continuity. They support testing the proposed entry against existing workflows; they do not justify abandoning the laboratory destination or declaring a winner from product descriptions.

**8090 is relevant to inherited systems, not just new software.** In a vendor-published CMS ClaimsCore account, 8090 describes extracting business rules from Assembly and COBOL, tracing them to source and policy documentation, matching rules across systems and supplying a reviewer application. It reports an 18-million-line scope and work to identify more than 100,000 rules. The page identifies 8090 as one of several contractors, disclaims CMS endorsement and labels the displayed code illustrative. This is evidence of a reported engagement and approach, not independently verified completeness, accuracy, productivity or proof that the standard Software Factory product performs the whole engagement unaided. [8090 customer account](https://www.8090.ai/customer-stories/cms-claimscore). Source-linked understanding and institutional-knowledge recovery therefore belong in the competitive comparison too.

**Separate available workflow from roadmap.** 8090's documentation describes Drift Bot reporting contradictions and uncovered changes against requirements/blueprints, with a failed status for detected drift or a failed run. Its public roadmap lists the Tests module as in development, Tests CI integration as planned and missions/mission control as exploratory. These distinctions matter: a documented drift check is neither an executable business-behavior test nor evidence that the broader testing roadmap has shipped. Confirm availability before a pilot. [Drift Bot](https://docs.8090.ai/codebase/overview), [roadmap](https://docs.8090.ai/resources/roadmap).

**The next comparison should test contracts and outcomes, not category names.** Choose the strongest feasible existing workflow for a willing team's actual problem, then compare it with the smallest WF addition. Include these questions where consequential:

- What exact code, requirements, configuration and evaluator revision does a result cover, and which changes invalidate it?
- Can the worker alter acceptance criteria, evaluator inputs or the reported result? Separate contexts or model families do not establish an enforced boundary.
- Does evaluation demonstrate the required behavior and reject plausible false completions, while also accepting valid work? Record unsupported cases, abstentions and false rejections.
- Can another maintainer recover the obligation, decision and evidence, correct stale knowledge and continue through a different executor? Verify export, ownership and deployment constraints rather than inferring them from marketing.
- What happens to total owner effort, setup, review, rescues, evaluator upkeep and later repair? Comparable quality at lower effort can be valuable without a higher defect-detection rate.

These are proposed qualification questions, not findings that any named competitor lacks the properties. This desk pass established neither a common independent performance benchmark nor customer preference. WF should consider composing an existing review product before building a replacement, and compare continuity against an existing control plane such as 8090. A standalone assurance offer, an integrated WF capability and a supported takeover workflow remain hypotheses for the operator to choose between using field evidence.

## What appears to work—and where it has not

### Productivity depends on the task, person and workflow

**Controlled evidence, June 2025:** three field experiments covering 4,867 developers reported a pooled 26.08% increase in completed tasks with an AI coding assistant; individual experiments were noisy and less experienced developers showed larger gains. This studies code completion and task throughput, not present-day autonomous modernization or long-term maintainability. [Microsoft-affiliated study](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-effects-of-generative-ai-on-high-skilled-work-evidence-from-three-field-experiments-with-software-developers/).

**Controlled evidence, early 2025 tools:** METR's study of 16 experienced open-source developers and 246 tasks in familiar repositories found tasks took 19% longer with AI access. It does not imply all developers or current agents are slower. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089).

**Follow-up with substantial limitations, February 2026:** METR explicitly says its newer experiment gives an unreliable estimate of current productivity effects because participation and task selection changed, alongside measurement difficulties. Treat the earlier result as a context-specific finding, not a timeless verdict; the update does not establish a dependable replacement speedup. [METR update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/).

**Observational evidence, July 2026 preprint:** a Microsoft rollout study reports roughly 24% more merged PRs among CLI-agent adopters over four months. Adoption is not random and PR count is an output proxy. This is relevant evidence of changed activity, not a causal estimate of useful engineering capacity or reduced maintenance cost. [Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01418).

**WF implication:** measure the whole workflow with the actual beneficiary. Separate familiar-maintainer tasks from takeover tasks, mechanical changes from ambiguous changes, and accepted outcomes from generated output. A benchmark win or aggregate productivity percentage cannot choose the product boundary.

### Repeatable transformations can beat free-form generation

**Operator report, April 2026:** Uber describes migrating over 75,000 test classes in four months using OpenRewrite, build-system integration, coexistence and incremental rollout. Initial failures led to added recipes or reverting affected files. Its attempt to generate migrations across several test files with AI was unsuccessful; AI was useful for debugging. This is a particular JUnit/Bazel migration, not proof that deterministic tooling always wins or a controlled comparison of current models. [Uber engineering account](https://www.uber.com/eg/en/blog/junit-migration/).

**WF implication:** select the execution method by transformation type. Keep repeatable mechanics deterministic when suitable tooling exists; use agents for investigation and uncertain exceptions. Establish whether behavior was preserved independently of whether the patch compiled. An old pattern may encode a business obligation that should survive modernization.

### Production agents benefit from the engineering platform around them

**Operator report, February 2026:** Stripe reports more than 1,000 merged agent-produced PRs a week, with human review. Its account emphasizes prepared development environments, deterministic workflow steps around agent reasoning, scoped context, fast checks and bounded CI retries. This demonstrates an operating approach in a mature internal platform, not independently measured ROI or a workload every small team can reproduce. [Stripe Minions](https://stripe.dev/blog/minions-stripes-one-shot-end-to-end-coding-agents).

**Vendor engineering experiments, November 2025:** Anthropic describes agents attempting too much, leaving incomplete work across sessions or prematurely declaring completion. Its response combines environment initialization, incremental work, testing and explicit progress artifacts. These are reported failure modes and design lessons, not universal laws about newer models. [Long-running harnesses](https://www.anthropic.com/research/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents).

**WF implication:** a product should supply a small usable slice of those conditions for its target team. Existing tests, runnable environments and fast feedback are prerequisites to qualify, not free infrastructure to assume. Where they are absent, the first valuable deliverable may be reliable behavioral evidence and a reproducible environment rather than faster patch generation.

### A merged change is not the end of the cost

**Observational preprint, September 2026:** a study followed 6,774 merged agent PRs and 5,044 contemporaneous human PRs in the same open-source repositories. Agent PRs had 1.62 times the odds of verified follow-up fixes; agents also authored much of the fixing. This is an association, not randomized causal evidence or a defect-rate multiplier. Selection, task mix and attribution limit transfer to enterprise systems. [Follow-up fixes study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26847).

**WF implication:** include follow-up repair, review, maintenance and handover in the outcome. Do not presume every repair is human toil, or that agent-authored repair is free. Report the observation window and unresolved exposure; a short pilot cannot establish years of reliability.

### Agents overclaim completion, and long-horizon success is rare

Added 2026-10-30. These sources quantify a failure the earlier evidence only described.

**Academic preprint, September 2026:** agents asked to review a set of files failed to read all of them in 67.9% of runs, and in those incomplete runs the agent's report was misleading 80.4% of the time (59% to 96% by model). Agents that falsely claimed a complete review missed planted defects about 1.8 times as often as agents that read everything. [Overclaiming propensity study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20812). This measures a review task, not a code change, and the planted defects were constructed.

**Academic benchmark, NeurIPS 2026:** on twenty long-horizon engineering tasks with multi-layer verification, frontier agents solved fewer than 20%, and 18.8% of attempts showed reward hacking, meaning the agent exploited the environment or the verifier. Named failure modes were poor self-verification, wrongly declaring a task infeasible and stopping early. [SWE-Marathon](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/139342). The abstract does not name the agents; results are for the benchmark's tasks and verifiers.

**Academic study, MSR 2026:** across 23,247 agent pull requests, descriptions that did not match the code were accepted 28.3% of the time against 80.0% for matching ones, and took 3.5 times longer to merge; the commonest mismatch claimed work the code did not contain. [Phantom-changes study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04886). A separate study of 567 agent PRs found 83.8% accepted against 91.0% for human PRs, with rejections mostly about project context rather than defects. [Acceptance study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14745).

**What is still missing:** no independent human-intervention or rescue rate for any named product on multi-day or multi-repository missions was found, and published merge rates for Devin, Codex and Copilot differ by source and method (61% to 93%), so they cannot be compared. No controlled evidence shows that persistent agent memory in Cursor, Claude Code, Codex or Windsurf improves accepted outcomes.

**WF implication:** acceptance that is independent of the worker, bound to the exact revision and tested against planted false completions targets a measured failure, not a hypothetical one. It also means the worker's own report cannot be the evidence of record. These figures are baselines for designing the H1 demonstration; they are not Wavefoundry results.

### Standards convergence and spec-driven frameworks

Added 2026-10-30. The Agentic AI Foundation, a Linux Foundation fund launched in December 2025, hosts MCP, AGENTS.md and A2A; on 2026-10-22 Anthropic donated Agent Skills (`SKILL.md`), alongside OpenAI Open Responses, Hugging Face OpenEnv and Alibaba OpenSandbox. The foundation says more than seventy agent platforms support Skills. [Foundation announcement](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox), [coverage](https://sdtimes.com/aaif/61588/).

**WF implication:** standard formats reduce differentiation from multi-host instruction delivery alone; their governance does not prove that complete workflow harnesses are commodities. Spec-driven frameworks compete on workflow, while the review products and 8090 above also compete on acceptance, continuity and composition. WF must demonstrate a useful outcome beyond the strongest feasible combination of those tools, rather than treating any of these categories as an uncontested position.

### Composable code intelligence

Added 2026-10-30. SCIP is a mature, language-agnostic index format for definitions, references and implementations, now maintained at [scip-code/scip](https://github.com/scip-code/scip); practitioner projects pair tree-sitter symbol extraction with SCIP to upgrade heuristic edges to compiler-verified ones. Several open-source code-graph MCP servers built on tree-sitter and SQLite appeared in 2026, such as [Codebase Memory MCP](https://landscape.jimmysong.io/projects/codebase-memory-mcp/) and [mcp-server-tree-sitter](https://pypi.org/project/mcp-server-tree-sitter/0.4.0/); a practitioner report says GitHub's stack-graphs is no longer maintained. Licenses, maturity and local-only viability of these projects were not checked.

**WF implication:** Wavefoundry's graph extractor (`graph_indexer.py`, about 17,600 lines, with query, clustering, snapshot and handler modules bringing the graph code to roughly 44,000 lines) supports Python by AST and fifteen programming languages by tree-sitter, with some call edges heuristic. Before extending it further, compare composing a SCIP indexer or an existing graph server behind the current query contract. This is a comparison to run, not a decision.

### Audit readiness has dates and no named artifact

Added 2026-10-30. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, entered into force on 2026-07-27 and moved obligations for standalone high-risk systems to 2027-12-02 and for systems embedded in regulated products to 2028-08-02; practitioner commentary holds that a coding agent is generally not itself a high-risk system. [Law-firm summary](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/07/27/the-digital-omnibus-on-ai-enters-into-force-today-102nedo). Practitioners argue SOC 2's change-management criterion applies identically to agent-made changes, so authorization, testing and approval evidence is required either way. No AICPA, ISO or EU text names an agent-change acceptance artifact; an individual IETF draft on signed agent audit trails has not been adopted. [Practitioner note](https://www.mintmcp.com/blog/build-audit-trails-ai-coding-agents), [IETF draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-sharif-agent-audit-trail/).

**WF implication:** the evidence package is relevant to existing change-management controls now, without waiting for AI-specific regulation. Audit readiness still requires qualification with a named recipient; no standard will do that for us.

## Practices worth carrying into the product

These are design recommendations synthesized from the evidence and practitioner sources. They are not an assertion that a specific combination has been experimentally validated for WF.

1. **Start modernization with the desired outcome and preserve useful behavior.** Cartwright, Horn and Lewis describe repeated unfinished replacement programs and advocate incremental displacement around outcomes, seams and organizational change. [Patterns of Legacy Displacement](https://martinfowler.com/articles/patterns-legacy-displacement/). For WF: map the owner, obligation and business reason for a change before generating a migration plan. Prefer coexisting, replaceable slices when practical; do not turn “newer technology” into a sufficient business case.
2. **Make readiness visible and proportional.** Qualify the environment, relevant dependencies and checks for the selected task. Distinguish a missing test from a passing test and unknown behavior from intended behavior. Ship a bounded useful improvement rather than demanding an enterprise-wide model before any work can begin.
3. **Use small changes and feedback from real operation.** Google SRE describes canaries, reproducible release artifacts and evaluation against reliability objectives; production can expose defects absent from test environments. [Canarying Releases](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/). For WF: link candidate, actual deployment and observation, with named decision/recovery owners. A green test receipt is not a production guarantee; some data changes need compensation rather than rollback.
4. **Treat adoption as organizational work.** DORA's 2025 report characterizes AI as amplifying strengths and weaknesses of the surrounding system. [Report](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/). For WF: reduce workflow friction, establish owner participation and fund ongoing integrations. A self-service tool may need an initial supported engagement; measure that service labor rather than hiding it in setup.
5. **Measure outcomes, not agent activity.** DORA's 2026 guidance warns against treating token consumption as productivity. It is guidance, not a controlled evaluation of every reported example. [Measurement discussion](https://dora.dev/insights/finding-balance-in-the-era-of-tokenmaxxing/). For WF: measure sustained useful changes, all-person effort, quality, maintainability and team experience. Agent count, tokens, PRs and completed review forms are diagnostic activity measures.
6. **Keep the harness changeable.** Anthropic reports a model-specific workaround becoming unnecessary with a later model, alongside its move to separable runtime interfaces. [Managed Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/managed-agents). For WF: periodically remove obsolete ceremony and retest controls against actual failures. Preserve project authority and evidence semantics while allowing tools, prompts and execution providers to change.

## How Wavefoundry should navigate the space

**Enter through a consequential recurring problem.** The proposed first beneficiary is an incoming or overstretched team responsible for an existing application. Candidate work includes a bounded dependency migration, an integration replacement or debt blocking a needed change. Choose one observed problem; do not sell an entire autonomous department before proving it helps.

**Demonstrate continuity as an engineering outcome.** The meaningful proof is that an unfamiliar maintainer can recover the intent, locate obligations, finish interrupted work, challenge stale evidence and perform a subsequent change. Generated documentation is an input. If a short handover convention and the team's preferred agent achieve the same result more cheaply, that is a valid reason to simplify or stop the product expansion.

**Combine rather than duplicate capabilities.** A candidate workflow is: establish the obligation and owner → inspect the relevant system slice → choose a recipe, agent or human method → deliver a bounded candidate → evaluate behavior → observe authorized release → preserve the decisions and useful evidence. Existing catalogs, analyzers, codemods, agents, CI and observability should supply their established capabilities. WF's added value must be measured at the connections. There is no claim that no other product connects them.

**Make knowledge useful and contestable.** Retain provenance, revision, unresolved questions and invalidation conditions; let an owner correct inferred knowledge. Don't create competing sources of authority. A durable chat log can help communication but cannot by itself establish current truth, permissions or acceptance. Protocol compatibility likewise does not guarantee equivalent cancellation, isolation, recovery or export semantics across providers.

**Preserve the destination while testing the means.** Department-scale capacity through an autonomous engineering laboratory includes enterprise stewardship. Continued research can favor different execution boundaries, integrations or operating models. An assurance-only or continuity-only destination remains an explicit strategic alternative if evidence warrants reconsideration; missing proof alone does not select it. Each expansion must justify its cost and responsibility without quietly replacing the destination with its first useful increment.

| Horizon | Implication of this research | Evidence needed next |
|---|---|---|

Installation discovery and cross-agent handover are already working in the operator's experience and should be baselines to extend. Enterprise access, representative data, owner availability and support economics remain on the critical path alongside technical work.

## Hostile inputs are part of the takeover problem

[OWASP's prompt-injection prevention guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html), checked on 2026-10-29, identifies repository comments, documentation and issues as indirect-input channels. It recommends layered defenses including tool-boundary authorization and least privilege; screening and a second model do not establish safety. For WF, keep untrusted content and inferred memory distinct from authority, protect acceptance, test data egress and constrain dependency/build execution. These are proposed controls and qualification questions, not verified properties of WF. Dependency execution also needs supply-chain controls independent of whether a prompt injection succeeds.

## Downstream experience and design-partner selection

The [recorded downstream lifecycle need](docs/waves/1zlu1%20lifecycle-features-for-distributions/1zlu2-enh%20close-one-change-and-activate-dependents.md) describes Waveforge using waves with independently finishing changes. The [session handoff](docs/agents/session-handoff.md) also records field upgrade feedback and its untested paths; this is a dated operating record, not a customer study, and generic field reports should not all be attributed to Waveforge. These sources support bounded downstream use and usability feedback. They do not establish demand for inherited-system takeover, productivity gains or willingness to pay.

Waveforge is a possible route to a design partner, subject to a willing team, a suitable inherited application, accountable owners and permitted data/access. No partner has been recruited or consent inferred. Discovery should select a real consequential mission and test whether an evidence package reduces decision/review effort against the team's current practice. Structured records are not yet authenticated, complete or tamper-resistant enterprise assurance. Any audit-readiness claim needs qualification for the recipient's actual requirements; no such qualification ran here.

## Next evidence and stopping rules

**Discovery first:** examine recently completed, abandoned and painful changes with a willing team. Obtain artifacts and actual effort where permitted. Include an incoming maintainer, an experienced system owner and the person responsible for cost or operational consequences. Establish whether the pain is understanding, execution, review, access, coordination or something else. Public sources cannot answer that for the target customer. Record candidate-team participation, missing prerequisites, refusal reasons, permission delays and readiness labor at an appropriately private aggregate level, without imposing a recruitment quota. Distinguish conditions the partner already had from those WF or specialists supplied. Report mechanism results separately from adoption evidence; if readiness support dominates, compare a supported engagement, an embedded component or internal-platform delivery before expanding commercial claims.

**Then a bounded comparison:** identify a consequential gap beyond the working installation and handover capabilities, and select one recurring class and define acceptance, cost accounting and stopping conditions before running it. Keep the model/executor, task difficulty, environment and evaluators comparable when isolating WF's contribution. Compare the strongest feasible existing workflow with that workflow plus the minimal WF capability. Do not compare WF's best integrated setup with an artificially weak bare agent.

Record eligible tasks, exclusions and reasons, and distinguish outgoing-owner assistance from unassisted takeover. Pin the recipe/executor versions, supported input conditions, acceptance owner and response to unsupported inputs. Repeatability does not establish preservation of business meaning.

**Expand only for a distinct question:** after a viable initial slice, test an unfamiliar maintainer and both mechanical and semantically ambiguous changes before generalizing to enterprise modernization. A transformation-only win does not prove takeover capacity. An evidence-poor system should test whether WF helps establish enough knowledge to act, or correctly identifies a blocking unknown; confident completion is not required at any cost.

**Count the full result:** onboarding, review, evaluator upkeep, support, rescues, failures, abstention, compute and later repair. Keep useful accepted-outcome coverage comparable so that avoiding difficult tasks cannot masquerade as productivity. Separate reusable setup from recurring cost while reporting both.

**Return to the operator at a finite boundary:** if the predeclared batch fails its outcome, a repair fails its recheck, required access is unavailable or the team declines repeat use, present the evidence and options before broadening. No automatic sequence of trials until a favorable result appears. No such pilot ran during this research.

Revisit this landscape before provider selection, authority expansion or a commercial claim. Customer demand, willingness to pay, integration friction, representative long-term quality and the best execution-ownership boundary remain unresolved. The current public research is sufficient to sharpen those questions and design the next bounded comparison; it is not proof that the proposed product will win.
